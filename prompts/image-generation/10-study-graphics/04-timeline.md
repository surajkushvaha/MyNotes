# Timeline

- **Category:** Image generation
- **Tutorial:** Study anything in 10 formats
- **Type:** prompt
- **Description:** Study graphic format: Timeline. Replace the topic (how a rocket reaches orbit) with yours.
- **Source:** https://learn.skaleout.com/study

```text
Create an image. A history timeline about how a rocket reaches orbit, as an editorial page on warm cream paper with serif headings: a vertical line with 8 entries in date order. Each entry has a small painted illustration on one side of the line and, on the other, a coloured year chip, a bold heading and two lines of text about what happened. A title and subtitle at the top, a one-line footer at the bottom.

Make it a portrait image with a 2:3 ratio. The image is the page itself: flat, edge to edge, not a photo of a poster, no desk, no hands, no frame around it. Make it look like a finished page from a premium illustrated study guide: full, with no big empty areas. Write real, accurate content about the topic: real facts and real numbers, no filler text and nothing made up. Small text is fine, but every word must be crisp and spelled correctly. One consistent illustration style, careful alignment. No logos, brand names or watermark.
```
