# Stained Glass

- **Category:** Animation loops
- **Tutorial:** A baby dragon in ten styles
- **Type:** prompt
- **Description:** Style: Stained Glass. Paste into a new Claude or ChatGPT chat.
- **Source:** https://learn.skaleout.com/video/dragon

```text
Create one self-contained HTML file that plays a 6 second looping animation in a 9:16 frame (a 1080x1920 canvas scaled to fit the window, black outside it). Draw everything in code: no images, no external files. For a 3D style you may load three.js from cdnjs.cloudflare.com; nothing else external.

Scene: what if a baby dragon took its first flight? One baby dragon flies over a cosy little town. Make the dragon cute: a chubby round body, one smooth rounded head with a short snout and no separate nose bulb, calm almond shaped eyes with heavy lids and small brows, a closed confident little smile, stubby horns, little wings flapping hard. Make it cool, not goofy: a confident swoop across the frame, a puff of sparkly smoke trailing off its tail, the town below with tiny people, sheep and birds looking up at it.

Style: a cathedral stained glass window, jewel coloured glass panes, thick dark lead lines, light glowing through the glass. Commit to this style completely: palette, shapes, line, texture, lighting and the way things move should all feel like it.

Motion: smooth, driven by requestAnimationFrame, a gentle camera drift, at least three layers of parallax, something moving at every moment, and a seamless loop every 6 seconds. No text on screen. Reply with only the HTML file.
```
