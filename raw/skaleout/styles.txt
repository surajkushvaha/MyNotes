style: Doodle
Copy
Create one self-contained HTML file that plays a 4 second seamless looping animation in a 9:16 frame (a 1080x1920 canvas scaled to fit the window, black outside it). Draw everything in code with the Canvas 2D API: no images, no external files, no libraries.

Subject: a spinning ball made of thousands of small marks (streaks, characters, hairs, dots or lines, whatever fits the style), centred a little above the middle and filling about 80 percent of the width. It rotates one full turn every 4 seconds about a tilted axis, so the marks on its front flow in one direction across the surface and the outline stays a perfect circle. Only draw the front half of the sphere, so it reads as a solid ball that is clearly spinning.

Style: a cartoon doodle mural: a pink ball with thick black outlines, spots and little eyes, on cyan packed with hand-drawn creatures, lines wobbling like hand-drawn frames. Commit to this style completely: the ball's material, the background, the palette and the texture should all feel like it. Fill the background with a full scene in that style, not a flat colour, and give it its own subtle motion that also loops every 4 seconds.

No text on screen. Reply with only the HTML file.

Want something other than a ball? Change the Subject paragraph: a planet, a donut, your logo on a coin. Keep the 4 second turn as it is: it makes the loop work.

Step by step
Open Claude or ChatGPT

Go to claude.ai or chatgpt.com and start a new chat.

Tip Free accounts may work. A paid plan is faster and handles long answers better.

Pick, copy, paste, send

Pick a style above, tap Copy, paste it into the chat and send it.

Tip It can think for several minutes. The answer is one HTML file, so let it finish.

Preview it

In Claude the HTML shows up as an artifact you can watch right in the chat. In ChatGPT open it in the canvas preview if it shows one.

Tip No preview? Copy the code into a file named ball.html and open it in your browser.

Black screen or an error?

Type "fix the error" in the same chat, or simply send the prompt again in a new chat.

Tip That is normal. Some styles need a second try.

Turn it into a video

Make the browser window tall (or use your phone) and screen record about 4 seconds: on a Mac press Cmd+Shift+5, on Windows press Win+Alt+R (Xbox Game Bar) or use the Snipping Tool's record, on iPhone or Android use the built-in screen recorder. Then crop it to 9:16 in the Instagram editor or CapCut.

Tip It loops every 4 seconds, so any 4 seconds work.

Post it

Post the loop on its own, or put several inside your own reel the way Jan did with ten styles.

Tip Run the same style twice and keep the better one. Every run is a new drawing.

Questions
Why does it take minutes?

The chat writes a whole animation program from scratch, often hundreds of lines of code, and plans it before it writes. A few minutes is normal. Keep the tab open until it is done.

Does it cost money?

The prompt and this tutorial are free. Claude and ChatGPT both have free plans you can try. Free plans have limits and may stop long answers early, so a paid plan works more reliably. There is nothing else to buy or install.

Can I use it commercially?

The animation code the chat writes for you is yours to post, edit and use. Check each tool's terms for the details that apply to your plan.

Can I change the subject?

Yes. Rewrite the Subject paragraph with anything you like and keep the rest of the prompt. Pick a style above or type your own with "Write your own".

Why does my ball look different?

Every run is a new drawing. The same prompt gives a different ball each time, even in the same style. The clips above also took a few rounds of fixes, so expect your first take to be simpler. Send it again for another take, or tell the chat what to change, for example "make the background busier" or "use more, finer strokes on the ball".
