Write real, accurate content about the topic: real facts and real numbers, no filler text and nothing made up. Small text is fine, but every word must be crisp and spelled correctly. Step by step

  1. Open ChatGPT

    Go to chatgpt.com and sign in. A free account works.

    Tip Free accounts can make fewer images per day. If it says you reached the limit, try again later.

  2. Pick a format and type your topic

    On this page tap a format, then type what you want to study in the topic field.

    Tip A short, clear topic works best, for example "how vaccines work" or "the water cycle".

  3. Copy, paste and send

    Tap Copy, open a new chat in ChatGPT, paste the prompt and send it.

    Tip Start a new chat for each topic, so nothing from an earlier chat gets mixed in.

  4. Wait for the image

    ChatGPT draws the image in the chat. It can take a minute or two.

    Tip Do not send the prompt again while it works. If nothing shows up after a few minutes, send it again in a new chat.

  5. Fix a misspelled word

    Read every label. If a word is wrong, reply in the same chat: "fix the spelling of WORD, keep everything else the same".

    Tip Put the wrong word in place of WORD, and fix one word at a time.

  6. Download it and use it

    Open the image and use the download button on it. Then add it to your notes, a slide or a post.

    Tip Want the same topic in another format? Paste a different prompt in the same chat and change nothing else.

Questions

Does it cost money?

The prompts and this tutorial are free. ChatGPT has a free plan, but it limits how many images you can make each day. A paid plan raises the limit. There is nothing else to buy or install.

Can I use it for school or work?

Yes, the images you make are yours to use as study material or in a presentation. Check OpenAI's terms for what applies to your plan. Check the facts too: an image model can get a detail wrong, so compare the labels with a trusted source.

Why does mine look different from the examples?

Every run makes a new image, and a different topic changes what is drawn. The examples show one run each. If you do not like yours, send the prompt again in a new chat, or reply with what to change, for example "make the labels bigger".

Can I use another topic or language?

Yes, any topic works. For another language, add a line at the end of the prompt, for example "Write all labels in Spanish". Spelling can be less reliable in other languages, so read every label.

Can I make all ten at once?

Yes. Send the ten prompts one by one in the same chat, one after the other, and wait for each image before you send the next.
